- Decision: ITERATE
- Justification: The current results have successfully established the existence and robustness of the superdiffusive regime ($\alpha \approx 1.9$), effectively ruling out finite-time artifacts and Lévy flights. However, the paper currently lacks a definitive physical mechanism to explain *why* this superdiffusion occurs. The proposed improvements—specifically decomposing the MSD into parallel/perpendicular components relative to the vorticity vector and correlating vortex velocity with the background fluid velocity—are not "unnecessary complexity." Instead, they are essential for distinguishing between passive advection by the large-scale forcing and intrinsic vortex dynamics. These additions will elevate the manuscript from a statistical observation to a mechanistic physical study, significantly increasing its "Acceptance Delta" for top-tier fluid dynamics or turbulence journals. The ROI is high because the data is already processed; the remaining work is analytical and will provide the "So What?" factor required for a high-impact publication.
