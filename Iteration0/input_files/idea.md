**Title**: Multiscale Statistical Mapping of Lagrangian Vortex Diffusion in 3D Isothermal Turbulence

**Description**: This study quantifies vortex transport regimes by processing the 1001-snapshot AthenaK dataset through a robust, noise-mitigating pipeline. To ensure physical validity, velocity fields are pre-processed with a Gaussian low-pass filter ($\sigma=1.0$ voxel) to suppress numerical artifacts before computing the velocity gradient tensor $\nabla \mathbf{v}$ and the Q-criterion. Vortices are identified via an adaptive, local-thresholding scheme ($Q > \text{local\_mean}(Q) + 3\sigma_{\text{local}}$) to account for spatial intermittency, with only structures persisting for $\geq 10$ timesteps retained to filter transient noise. We track these persistent vorticity-weighted centroids using a greedy nearest-neighbor approach optimized for periodic boundaries via the minimum-image convention. Statistical characterization is performed by calculating the Mean Squared Displacement (MSD) $\langle |r(t+\tau) - r(t)|^2 \rangle \propto \tau^\alpha$ and fitting step-size distributions to Gaussian and Lévy stable models. The final analysis integrates 3D trajectory visualizations, log-log MSD plots, and Kolmogorov-Smirnov goodness-of-fit tests to isolate the diffusion exponent $\alpha$ and determine whether vortex trajectories exhibit Brownian motion or Lévy-flight superdiffusion within the resolved inertial range.
